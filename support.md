# Caliper Support

Caliper films a set and measures each rep against your own range of
motion. If something is not working, or a result looks wrong, write
to us.

**Email:** calipersupport@gmail.com

It helps to include the exercise, what you expected, and what the
app showed. Please do not send videos by email; the app has a
"Send feedback" option under Help & settings that can attach a set
video.

## Common questions

**The result says no reps could be measured.** The camera needs
your whole body in frame, head to feet, for the whole set. Prop the
phone upright and still, 2 to 3 m away, at the angle the Record
screen shows for that exercise, and film the set again.

**My reps are not compared with a range yet.** For supported
exercises, Caliper learns your range from your first two analyzed
sets. Those first results show measurements only and say so. From
the third analyzed set, each rep shows Reached your range, Near
boundary, or Below your range. Other exercises show rep counts and
angles with no comparison; the Record screen marks which are which.

**A rep says "Below your range" and I think it was fine.** Open the
result and tap "Watch set video" to see the set. The usual causes
are the phone at a different angle or distance from your first sets,
or part of your body leaving the frame on that rep. Near boundary
reps still count toward progress; only Below your range and Unusual
reps are left out, and both are always shown.

**How long does analysis take?** About a minute when the server is
free. The server measures one video at a time, so if other people
are analyzing at the same moment it can take several minutes. The
upload finishes first, and analysis continues if you leave the
screen or close the app.

**The row says "Upload interrupted".** Your recording is still on
your phone. Tap Retry upload; it sends the same set again and does
not create a copy. If it keeps failing, check your connection and
try again later.

**Analysis says to try again tomorrow.** There is a daily limit on
analyses per phone. It is a rolling 24-hour window, so the limit
clears 24 hours after the earliest analysis that counted toward it.

**Deleting your data.** Videos stay on your phone and are removed
from the server after analysis. See the
[privacy policy](https://latixnn.github.io/lift-privacy/) for the
one exception and how to request deletion.

## How Caliper is validated

Caliper measures movement from video using pose tracking. An
exercise is compared with your personal range only after its
measurement has been validated on filmed sets that were not used to
tune it: at least 100 honest reps held out from development with no
false flags, plus deliberately shortened reps that the measurement
caught. Four exercises have passed that bar so far: squat, leg
extension, shoulder press and pec deck. The others are measured and
shown, but not compared, until they pass the same test. Caliper
does not assess technique and does not give training advice; it
compares you with your own earlier sets, nothing else. It is not a
medical device and nothing in it is medical advice; the programs and
the add-weight suggestion are a training log, not a judgment about
your health.
